## E. What transcribing your own film revealed

The audio is now transcribed (45 segments). **The deck significantly understates your technology,
and three things in it are factually wrong.** All of the below is your own narration, so it is the
most authoritative source in this project.

### E1. The drying technology — now the centrepiece

Slide 6 used to say *"controlled hot-air drying"*. It is now titled **"Catalytic infrared
dehydration"** and carries the real story, all of it from your own narration:

- A **European catalytic infrared (IR) dehydrator**
- **Platinum metal-plated** chamber walls, ceiling and doors, so IR reflects from every direction
  and each nut dries uniformly from all angles
- **Infrared heat-radiating metal infusors** generating controlled IR waves
- A high-performance **compressor** drawing atmospheric air through a purpose-built
  **dehumidification** system
- **10 tonnes of material per 12 hours**, moisture reduced **below 10%**
- Against traditional sun drying: **five days** to reach only **below 20%**
- **Weather-independent** — production continues through rainfall, consistent year-round
- **~3,300 sq ft** drying area; claimed **among India's largest** coconut dehydration facilities
- PLC touchscreen holding temperature regardless of climate
- Output **~100,000 litres per month**
- **Sulfur-free, no chemicals** — the basis of the 100% organic claim
- Tagline: **"Pure in every drop."**

Sulfur-free matters commercially — sulfur is routine in copra drying and importers screen for it, so
it is a real differentiator. It now appears on slides 1, 3, 4 and 8.

### E2. Three factual corrections — all applied

1. **Washing happens before dehydration, not after paring.** Slide 4 now runs wash at step 4,
   dehydration at step 5. Slide 7 is retitled "Washing and inspection" with the wash image first and
   captioned "before dehydration".
2. **It is scooping, not paring.** Slide 7's old "hand paring and grading" caption is gone; the
   post-drying step is now "Scooping and pulverising" on slides 4 and 8, described as the kernel
   lifting cleanly away from the shell because it is thoroughly dried.
3. **No dehusking.** Slides 4 and 5 now say nuts feed into an automated conveyor cutting system
   that halves every nut evenly.

The eight-step flow on slide 4 is now: intake · automated cutting · trays and trolleys ·
high-pressure wash · infrared dehydration · tray inspection · scooping and pulverising ·
expelling and filtration.
