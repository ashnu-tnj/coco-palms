# COCO PALMS — source material notes

Derived from the company profile video. Everything below is read off the footage
itself; nothing here is assumed. Items marked *(uncertain)* were partly illegible.

## The brand

**Coco Palms Skylanes and Allied Products** — coconut processing / coconut oil manufacturer.

Logo (clean, full-frame, white background at ~4:45 — the best asset in the whole video):
- Twin palm trees above two coconut halves that form the letters "COCO"
- A golden oil droplet falling between them
- Cupped golden hands beneath, holding the mark
- `PALMS` in dark green serif; `SKYLANES` in a green gradient sans;
  `AND ALLIED PRODUCTS` in gold caps, flanked by rules

Palette read off the logo and packaging: dark forest green, bright leaf green,
gold/amber (the oil), coconut-husk brown, coconut-flesh cream, white.

Product on camera: a tall clear cylindrical bottle of coconut oil with the full
logo on a white label.

## Certifications

The closing card animates in three marks under the heading **OUR CERTIFICATIONS**:

1. **ISO 9001** ("Certified Company" seal)
2. **FSSAI**
3. **NABL**

Framed certificates on the office wall behind the founder include two **BRIT QUALIS**
certificates, a **National Test House** certificate, and an appreciation certificate
*(wall text is small and only partly legible)*.

## Process narrative implied by the footage

Plantation / intake → dehusking → splitting and deshelling → **controlled hot-air
drying** (the PLC panel and the chamber shots get by far the most screen time, so
this is positioned as the technical differentiator) → manual paring and grading →
shredding to desiccated coconut → oil extraction and filtration → filling and
bottling → quality accreditation.
