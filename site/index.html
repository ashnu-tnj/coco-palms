Who we are

Thirteen years of trade, behind a plant built for it.

Coco Palms by Skylanes and Allied Products has been trading in coconut and allied products since 2013. In 2026 we commissioned a purpose-built dehydration facility on our four-acre site in Chengalpattu district, Tamil Nadu.

Whole nuts are received at our own intake yard, cut and washed on site, dried in a catalytic infrared dehydrator, then pressed and filtered into finished oil — a drying plant specified to current food-safety standards rather than retrofitted to them.

How it is made

From whole nut to filtered oil

Eight stages, all of them on one site. The nut is opened where it is received and never leaves our control until it is filtered oil.

01Intake
  1. Step 01

    Intake

    Nuts arrive by tipper from growers and regional mandis across the Tamil Nadu delta, and are offloaded onto our own raw material yard by our uniformed crew.

  2. Step 02

    Automated cutting

    An automated conveyor cutting system halves every nut evenly, so the kernel presents a consistent surface to the dryer.

  3. Step 03

    Trays and trolleys

    Halves are arranged on stainless drying trays and loaded onto trolleys ready for the wash line.

  4. Step 04

    High-pressure wash

    A high-pressure water spray removes dust and impurities picked up in transit and handling. Water only — no chemical bath.

  5. Step 05

    Infrared dehydration

    Ten tonnes per twelve-hour batch, down to below 10% moisture, in a European catalytic infrared chamber.

  6. Step 06

    Tray inspection

    Every tray is checked by trained staff and supervisors before the kernel moves on. Anything off-spec comes out here.

  7. Step 07

    Scooping & pulverising

    Dried kernel is lifted clear of the shell and reduced to a uniform particle size for pressing.

  8. Step 08

    Expelling & filtration

    A mechanical expeller presses the oil, which then passes through advanced filtration to the release specification.

Step 05 · Our technical differentiator

Catalytic infrared dehydration

Drying decides the quality of the finished oil, so it is the step we have engineered hardest. Instead of sun drying we use a European catalytic infrared dehydrator — one of the fastest and most efficient methods of coconut dehydration available.

  • Infrared-radiating metal infusors generate controlled heat waves
  • Platinum metal-plated walls, ceiling and doors reflect heat from every direction
  • A compressor draws atmospheric air through a dehumidification system
  • Roughly 3,300 sq ft of drying area

Tested, not assumed.

Every manufacturing batch is tested by our quality assurance team in our own in-house laboratory before it reaches a customer.
